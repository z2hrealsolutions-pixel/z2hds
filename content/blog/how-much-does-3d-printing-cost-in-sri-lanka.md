People often ask for a price per gram or a price per hour. Those numbers sound simple, but they hide what really sets the cost. Here is what we look at when we price a job at Z2H Design Studio in Maharagama.

## The six things that set the price

1. **Material.** The weight of plastic in the part, and which plastic. PLA+ is the everyday choice. PETG, ABS/ASA and TPU cost different amounts.
2. **Print time.** Printers run for hours, and every hour has a cost. A tall, detailed part takes longer than a short, simple one.
3. **Colours.** A multi-colour part needs the printer to change colour many times, which adds time and wasted plastic. Read more in our guide to [multi-colour 3D printing](/blog/multi-colour-3d-printing-explained).
4. **Size.** Parts bigger than the printer are printed in pieces and joined. The joining adds work.
5. **Finishing.** Removing supports, sanding and cleaning take time. A display piece needs more finishing than a hidden bracket.
6. **How many.** Several copies of the same part get our quantity discounts, so each piece costs less than a single one on its own.

## A real example

A 4-colour figurine we priced recently came out at about 189 g of filament and 12.9 hours of print time. Our estimator gave a range of **LKR 9,600 to 14,600** for one piece in PLA with a basic finish.

It is a range, not one number, because the final figure depends on how the print is planned. We confirm the final price after we check your file.

## How to get a number for your own part

1. Open the [instant estimate](/estimate).
2. Upload an STL, 3MF or OBJ file.
3. Choose the material and how many you need.
4. See the range, then message us to confirm.

No file? Send a photo or a sketch on WhatsApp or through the [contact page](/contact). We can model it, and we will tell you the price for the modelling first.
