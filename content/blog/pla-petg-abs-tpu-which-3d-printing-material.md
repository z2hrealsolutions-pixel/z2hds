At Z2H Design Studio we print in PLA+, PLA, PLA variants, PETG, PETG variants, ABS/ASA, TPU and Lightweight PLA. You do not have to know which to pick. Tell us what the part will do and we will suggest one. This guide helps you understand the choice.

## The short table

| Material | Good for | Think twice if |
|---|---|---|
| PLA+ | Statues, gifts, models, most prototypes | The part will sit in a hot place |
| PLA | Models, decor, display pieces | The part must take knocks |
| PLA variants | A special look, such as a shiny or matte finish | You need extra strength |
| PETG | Functional parts, parts near water | You need a very fine, sharp surface |
| PETG variants | PETG strength with a different look | You only need a display piece |
| ABS/ASA | Parts that must handle heat; ASA outdoors | It is a quick display piece |
| TPU | Grips, seals, cases, anything flexible | The part must stay stiff |
| Lightweight PLA | Large props and display pieces | The part must carry a load |

## PLA+ and PLA

PLA+ is our everyday choice. It prints with good detail and a clean finish, and it is tougher than standard PLA. Standard PLA has the widest range of colours and suits models and decor. Neither is the best choice for a part that will sit in a hot car or in direct sun for long periods.

## PLA variants

PLA in special finishes and blends, chosen mostly for looks. Ask us which ones we have in stock for your job.

## PETG and PETG variants

Tougher than PLA, and it copes better with moisture and some heat. Good for functional parts such as brackets, covers and holders. PETG variants give the same toughness with a different look.

## ABS/ASA

Hard, sturdy plastics that handle heat better than PLA. We use them when a part needs to sit near warm places or take knocks. ASA also stands up to sunlight, so it suits outdoor parts. Both are harder to print well than PLA, so tell us early if your part needs them.

## TPU

A flexible, rubbery material. It bends and returns to shape, which makes it good for grips, seals, phone and device cases, and parts that absorb shock.

## Lightweight PLA

A PLA that prints with a lighter, foamed body. It is a good fit for large display pieces and costume props that people need to carry, because the finished part weighs less.
