At Z2H Design Studio we print in five materials: PLA+, PETG, ABS, TPU and Lightweight PLA. You do not have to know which to pick. Tell us what the part will do and we will suggest one. This guide helps you understand the choice.

## The short table

| Material | Good for | Think twice if |
|---|---|---|
| PLA+ | Statues, gifts, models, most prototypes | The part will sit in a hot place |
| PETG | Functional parts, parts near water | You need a very fine, sharp surface |
| ABS | Parts that must handle heat and knocks | It is a quick display piece |
| TPU | Grips, seals, cases, anything flexible | The part must stay stiff |
| Lightweight PLA | Large props and display pieces | The part must carry a load |

## PLA+

The everyday choice, and the one we use most. It prints with good detail and a clean finish, so it suits statues, vases, light-up letters and prototypes. It is not the best choice for a part that will sit in a hot car or in direct sun for long periods.

## PETG

Tougher than PLA, and it copes better with moisture and some heat. Good for functional parts such as brackets, covers and holders.

## ABS

A hard, sturdy plastic that handles heat better than PLA. We use it when a part needs to sit near warm places or take knocks. It is harder to print well than PLA, so tell us early if your part needs it.

## TPU

A flexible, rubbery material. It bends and returns to shape, which makes it good for grips, seals, phone and device cases, and parts that absorb shock.

## Lightweight PLA

A PLA that prints with a lighter, foamed body. It is a good fit for large display pieces and costume props that people need to carry, because the finished part weighs less.

## Not sure?

Tell us three things: what the part is, where it will be used, and whether it needs to bend or carry a load. We will pick the material and say why.
