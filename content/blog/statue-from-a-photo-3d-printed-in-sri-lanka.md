A custom statue is one of the most personal gifts you can give. At Z2H Design Studio, our figurine studio, **Realities by Z2H DS**, makes busts, full figures and couple statues from photos.

## The steps

1. **Send photos.** Use WhatsApp or the [contact page](/contact).
2. **We model the person.** We build a 3D model from your photos.
3. **You get a price.** After we check the photos and choose the size, we send a final price.
4. **Pay a 50% deposit.** Printing starts after that.
5. **We print and finish.** We clean, finish and check the statue against the model.
6. **Collect or deliver.** Collect from Maharagama, or we send it by PickMe Flash or Uber. You pay the balance before it leaves.

## Which photos to send

Good photos make a good statue. Please send:

- A clear photo from the **front**, and one from each **side**
- Good, even light, with no strong shadow across the face
- The face fully visible, with no sunglasses, and the hair clear of the eyes
- For a full figure, photos that show the whole body and the clothes
- For a couple, a photo of each person and, if you have it, a photo of them together

Blurry or very dark photos make it hard to capture the face. If your photos are not great, send what you have and tell us. We will say what is possible.

## What you can choose

- **Bust or full figure.** A bust shows head and shoulders. A full figure shows the whole person.
- **One colour or many.** A single colour looks like a classic statue. Multi-colour printing can show the skin, hair and clothes.
- **Size.** Up to about 25 cm in one piece. Bigger statues are printed in pieces and joined, which is how we make statues of 50 cm and more.
- **Extras.** Display cases and printed name plates.

## Good gifts for

Weddings, birthdays, awards, and bulk orders for a team or a company. We have printed a batch of identical busts for one bulk order, and each piece was checked one by one.
